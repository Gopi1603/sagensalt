## 📸 Photo Strategy

### Initial Upload (Do Once)
1. **Logo** (1:1 ratio, 720x720px minimum)
   - Clean logo on transparent/dark green background
   - High resolution

2. **Cover Photo** (16:9 ratio, 1920x1080px)
   - Interior ambience or signature dish hero shot
   - Shows restaurant personality

3. **Interior Photos** (Upload 10-15)
   - Seating areas
   - Dining space
   - Decor details
   - Bar/counter area (if applicable)
   - Clean, well-lit shots

4. **Exterior Photos** (Upload 3-5)
   - Storefront
   - Entrance
   - Signage
   - Street view for easy identification

5. **Food Photos** (Upload 20-30)
   - All signature dishes
   - Biryani variants (Chicken, Mutton, Veg)
   - Popular starters (Chicken 65, Paneer 65)
   - Chinese dishes
   - Plated presentations
   - Close-ups showing texture

6. **Kitchen/Hygiene** (Upload 5-8)
   - Clean kitchen workspace
   - Food preparation areas
   - Staff following hygiene protocols
   - FSSAI certificate displayed

7. **Team Photos** (Upload 3-5)
   - Chef/owner introduction
   - Staff in uniform
   - Team serving customers

### Weekly Photo Uploads (Every Week)
**Goal: Keep profile fresh and active**

- **Week 1**: New dish launches or daily specials
- **Week 2**: Behind-the-scenes kitchen prep
- **Week 3**: Happy customers (with permission) or ambience shots
- **Week 4**: Popular dishes from different angles

**Photo Tips:**
- Natural lighting preferred
- No filters (authentic look)
- Focus on food quality, not perfection
- Show portion sizes clearly
- Include garnishes and accompaniments

#### ⚠️ Negative Review (Service Issue)
```
Hi [Customer Name],

Sorry to hear about the wait time/service issue. We're reviewing our process to ensure this doesn't happen again.

Your feedback helps us improve. We'd appreciate the chance to serve you better next time.

- Sage N Salt Team
```

#### ⚠️ Neutral Review (3 stars)
```
Hi [Customer Name],

Thanks for trying us out. We'd love to know what we can improve. Feel free to reach out at 6300591870.

Hope to earn 5 stars next visit!

- Sage N Salt Team
```

### Review Response Guidelines
- Respond within 24 hours (ideally within 12 hours)
- Always thank the customer
- Address specific points mentioned
- Keep it brief (2-3 sentences)
- Never argue or get defensive
- For serious issues, take conversation offline
- Use customer's name when available
- Sign off consistently

- ❌ Stuffing business name with keywords (e.g., "Sage N Salt Best Biryani")
- ❌ Inconsistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across platforms
- ❌ Not responding to reviews
- ❌ Posting low-quality photos
- ❌ Ignoring Q&A section
- ❌ Setting wrong business hours
- ❌ Not updating for holidays/special hours
