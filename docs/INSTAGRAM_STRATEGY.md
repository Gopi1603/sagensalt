## 📖 Instagram Stories Strategy

### Daily Stories (Ephemeral Content)

**Story Types:**
1. **Morning Prep** (9-10 AM)
   - Show kitchen getting ready
   - Fresh ingredients arrival
   - "Good morning" greeting

2. **Lunch Rush** (12-2 PM)
   - Orders going out
   - Popular dishes of the day
   - "Order now" CTA with swipe-up

3. **Behind the Scenes** (3-5 PM)
   - Cooking process
   - Team at work
   - Q&A polls

4. **Dinner Specials** (7-9 PM)
   - Available items
   - "Still open" reminder
   - Call to order CTA

5. **Customer Reposts** (Anytime)
   - Repost customer tags
   - Reviews screenshots
   - Thank you messages

### Story Highlights (Permanent)

**Create these highlight categories on profile:**

1. **🍽️ MENU** - All dish photos organized
2. **⭐ REVIEWS** - Customer testimonials and ratings
3. **📍 LOCATION** - Directions, parking, hours
4. **🎉 SPECIALS** - Weekly offers and new launches
5. **👨‍🍳 KITCHEN** - Behind the scenes, hygiene
6. **📞 ORDER** - Links to Zomato, Swiggy, phone number
7. **🎂 CATERING** - Party orders and bulk catering info

## 🎨 Visual Style Guide

### Colors
- Dark forest green (#0d3b2f) - Borders, backgrounds
- Gold accent (#d4af37) - Text overlays, highlights
- Off-white (#f5f3ee) - Text on dark backgrounds

### Font Style
- Clean sans-serif for readability
- Elegant serif for special announcements

### Photo Guidelines
- Natural lighting preferred
- No heavy filters (maintain authenticity)
- Consistent framing and composition
- Show food texture and freshness
- Include brand elements (plates, packaging with logo)
