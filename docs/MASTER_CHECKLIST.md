## 🚨 AVOID THESE MISTAKES

### DON'T:
- ❌ Buy fake reviews
- ❌ Use keyword-stuffed business name
- ❌ Have inconsistent phone/address
- ❌ Ignore negative reviews
- ❌ Post low-quality photos
- ❌ Spam hashtags on Instagram
- ❌ Copy competitor content
- ❌ Use clickbait or false claims
- ❌ Forget to respond to customer messages
- ❌ Neglect mobile experience

### DO:
- ✅ Be authentic and transparent
- ✅ Focus on quality over quantity
- ✅ Respond promptly to all inquiries
- ✅ Keep information consistent everywhere
- ✅ Post regularly (consistency is key)
- ✅ Engage with your community
- ✅ Monitor and adapt based on data
- ✅ Ask satisfied customers for reviews
- ✅ Keep website fast and mobile-friendly
- ✅ Showcase real food and real experiences
